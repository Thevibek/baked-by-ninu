Our Story

Welcome to Baked by Ninu.

What started as a simple hobby soon turned into my true passion-and now, my profession. As a former graphic designer, creativity has always been at the heart of everything I do, and I love channeling that artistic vision into my work. Every challenge pushes me to grow, experiment, and create something truly special. I'm incredibly grateful for the inspiration I find every day through the amazing community on TikTok, Instagram, and Pinterest-their stories, recipes, and creativity continue to fuel my own journey. This isn't just a career for me; it's the life I've always dreamed of one where passion and artistry lead the way to a future where I can truly relax, enjoy, and do what I love.
